Look up an employer that files OSHA injury logs: its expected injury rate for next year against its industry, and where it sits against OSHA's Site-Specific Targeting criteria.

Plans and API keys: pricing. Scoring a whole book of business? Upload a CSV with name, state and ZIP columns through the batch endpoint in the API docs.

An independent project built on public OSHA injury logs and Department of Labor enforcement records. Not affiliated with OSHA or the Department of Labor.
